How it learns

Each step, the robot sees which of the four sides are open, whether each open side is new or already visited, and whether the goal is above, below, left or right. It learns what to do in each situation with Q-learning across thousands of random mazes, so the strategy carries over to new layouts. Blue shading shows cells it has visited.
